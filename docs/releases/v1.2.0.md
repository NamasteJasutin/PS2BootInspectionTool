## The PSX Boot Inspection Tool

The PS2 Boot Inspection Tool is now the **PSX Boot Inspection Tool** (the repository moved to `NamasteJasutin/PSXBootInspectionTool`; old links redirect). The PlayStation joins the PlayStation 2 in one app, both get research instruments, and a studio makes music with the BIOS's own instruments, and now with your games' instruments too. Nothing from Sony ships: every picture, sound and fact is derived at run time from **your** BIOS dumps, memory cards and disc images.

### The PlayStation
- Load any standalone PS1, PSone or PSP-POPS BIOS and the PS1 console boots its own shell. The SCE intro (diamond, logotype and its music) is rebuilt from the dump. The licence screen follows that shell's own licence policy, wordmark (® or ™) and SCEx label, read from its code rather than its version number.
- With no disc, the boot ends where the console does: in the shell's MAIN MENU (MEMORY CARD / CD PLAYER), drawn from the dump's own plates and labels. The redesigned menu of 3.0 E and the PSone 4.5 is shown as a caption for now.
- The Disc tab shows how the PS1 kernel reads `SYSTEM.CNF`. The first line starting with a key decides, so a `BOOT2` line before `BOOT` hides the PS1 boot file. The Lab adds the kernel's POST stages and the TTY log it would print.
- The BIOS tab identifies every dump (kernel K1/K2, shell storage, licence policy) and audits the set: duplicates, mislabelled files, text-mode-corrupted copies.

### PlayStation 2
- ROM 1.00 J (SCPH-10000) loads: its opening, its sounds and its NTSC-only logo.
- BIOS companions are read too: the NVM (decoded and checksum-validated), MEC, ROM1/ROM2 and EROM, and what each predicts.

### Three workspaces
- **Boot**: the scene, files, scenario, cameras, director, save data and towers.
- **Inspect**: BIOS, Disc, Lab and Fonts, for every console in one place.
- **Studio**: Music, Sound and Soundfonts. The Studio takes the focus; the boot pauses and picks up where it was when you leave.

### Lab
- For your BIOS, card, NVM and disc together: the hidden hand-overs the console looks for and whether they would fire, each with its ROM address. These include card system updates, the card DVD player, HDD boot, TESTMODE, the illegal-disc verdict and the 2.00 hidden Remote Control page.
- What a game asks of the console: SDK libraries, IOP modules and version gates.
- PS1 memory cards: undelete candidates and SoundScope performances.
- PS3 system-update snapshots and a PS3 disc/save metadata card. These read plaintext container structure only; nothing is decrypted.

### Camera and towers
- Director mode: keyframed camera scripts, built-ins, a seeded Shuffle, JSON import/export.
- Your card's own save icons float over their games' towers.

### Studio
- **Every instrument in your BIOS files**: the PS2 boot bank, the PS1 shell's bank, and the PS2 menu and logo effects (41 sounds), grouped by measured style. Drum kits (kick, snare, hats, clap, toms) are built by processing your own samples.
- **A DAW-style editor**: transport (Space plays/pauses), instrument browser, arranger with playhead and loop, piano roll, drum grid and inspector.
- **BIOS music**: import any of the 10 pieces in your BIOS as a starting song, such as the boot music, the clock and warning ambiences, the PS1 licence sting or the SCE intro.
- **Saving and export**: `.psxsong` files hold notes and recipes only, never audio. Export as WAV or the PS2 BIOS's own SSsq format.
- **Soundfonts from your games**: extract the sound banks from your own disc images into a Soundfonts folder, one set per disc in the native formats, with an index of where each bank came from. Supported: PS1 VAB (and split VH/VB), SEQ and VAG; PS2 SShd/SSsq, HD/BD and VAG; RIFF WAVE. Each bank is checked by parsing it, so look-alike bytes are left out. The Studio mixes game banks with the BIOS banks; each is named after its file on the disc and comes with its own derived drum kit. A source filter picks All, BIOS or one disc.
- **SoundFont 2 export** of any bank, BIOS or game, for other samplers and DAWs.
- **Sound and Fonts benches**: audition every program and effect, browse every glyph.

### PSX Studio
The Studio on its own: `psxstudio` opens straight into Music, Sound and Soundfonts without the boot scenes. On macOS it ships as *PSX Studio.app*.

### Command line
New tools next to the app: `extract` (disc banks into a Soundfonts folder), `library`, `song`, `bench` (now with `--sf2`), `nvminfo`, `hookmap`, `discprobe`, `romdiff`, `ps1card`, `pupinfo` and `ps3card`.

### Research and documentation
- Five research rounds in `notes/research/`: PS1/PSone/POPS kernels and shells, the version matrix, hidden controller input, how the BIOSes changed between versions, the complete BIOS sound inventory (5 banks, 41 instruments, 10 pieces of music), and the PS3 from public documentation.
- psdevwiki-ready entries in `docs/wiki/`.
- Specifications of the tool's own formats (`.psxsong`, PUP snapshots, drum-kit recipes) in `docs/formats/`.

### Library
The new `ps2kit` modules are on `main` (see `ps2kit/CHANGELOG.md`, *Unreleased*); crates.io stays at 0.3.0 until the next crate release.

### Downloads
One archive per platform below: Windows x64, Linux x64 and macOS arm64 (with *PSX Boot Inspection Tool.app* and *PSX Studio.app*). Requirements: your own BIOS dump(s), a PS2 ROM (1.00–2.00 verified) and/or a PS1, PSone or POPS BIOS. A PCSX2 memory card and disc images (`.iso`, or `.bin`/`.cue`) are optional.
