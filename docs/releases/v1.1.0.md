## What the console would do if…

The tool no longer only replays your files — it answers "what would happen if". Nothing from Sony ships with it: every picture, sound and fact is derived at run time from **your** BIOS dump, PCSX2 memory card and disc image.

### Scenario bar
- Pretend the console is J / A / E / C, tell the drive it found no disc or an illegal one, switch the region lock on or off.
- The full sequence follows the facts: a US disc on a European BIOS ends in the red warning scene as it does on the console; an empty tray ends at the clock/main menu; an illegal disc goes opening → warning. The hand-off card says which path and why.

### Save data tab
- Launch histogram, last-launches-over-time, the records with their dates.
- **Alternate history**: launch any title (known or new) exactly as the console's `HistoryUpdate` records it — a tower bit at the 14th/24th/34th… launch, a 22nd title evicting the least-played record (with the console's tie-break quirk), nothing recorded once every record is maxed. Undo, reset, export CSV.
- **Tower inspector**: hover a tower in the picture for its record; its towers glow.
- Beyond the PS2: towers rearranged on a **ring or spiral** that revolves, coloured by launch count, by how long ago the title was last played, by region or by publisher family.

### Cameras
- Predetermined paths around the city's axis — **orbit, tornado, crane, figure-eight, zenith** — with speed in degrees per frame, distance, aim point up the towers, height and lens controls. The towers' sides are lit so they read as solids off the console's own axis.
- The free camera and the paths also work in the second phase: the PS1 licence screen's logo is real 3D (the shell's lit triangles before projection), the PS2 logo hangs on a picture plane.

### Tabs, time, BIOS
- Sidebar tabs **Boot · Save data · Disc · BIOS**; a segment strip under the scrubber (click to jump), single-field stepping; the BIOS tab decodes `ROMVER` and lists the ROMDIR modules.
- Offline renderer: `--region`, `--tray`, `--lock`, `--layout`, `--tint`, `--revolve`, `--path`, `--degrees`, `--distance`, `--focus`, `--height`, `--zoom`.

### Research
- `notes/research/`: four surveys of the hidden hand-overs across five ROMs (1.00 J, 1.50 devkit, 1.60 A/E, 2.00 E with ROM1/ROM2/EROM/NVM/MEC) and four discs — OSDSYS hooks, kernel/EELOAD/IOP probes, what games ask the console, the NVM field map — with a merged, ranked list of research tooling.
- `docs/campaign.md` (the roadmap) and `docs/options.md` (64 options, with two dissents on record: no emulator inside the tool, no shipped artwork).

### Library
- `ps2kit` **0.3.0** on crates.io: `BootOutcome`/`BootPlan`, `Segment::{Warning, Menu}`, `HandoffStep::IllegalDisc`, `boot_outcome`, `RomDir::entries`, `PlayHistory::launch`, `Ps1Shell::lit_triangles`. See `ps2kit/CHANGELOG.md`.
